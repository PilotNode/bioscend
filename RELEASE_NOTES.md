# BioScend v1.0.0 Release Notes (Production) 🎉

We are thrilled to announce the official 1.0.0 production release of **BioScend**! 

BioScend is not just another tracker; it is your intelligent companion for optimizing supplement stacks, wellness routines, and longevity goals. With a focus on privacy, comprehensive data tracking, and cutting-edge AI analysis, this major initial release brings your biology and mind to the next level.

## What's New in 1.0.0

### 🧠 AI-Powered Insights (Gemini Integration)
BioScend now leverages advanced AI to understand your wellness data. It analyzes your daily logs, identifies patterns, and acts as your personal health data scientist, offering tailored recommendations on timing, dosages, and routine adjustments to maximize the efficacy of your supplement stacks.

### 📊 Smart Analytics & Dashboards
Visualize your progress with beautiful, reactive charts. 
* **Correlation Tracking**: Instantly see how your habits directly impact your wellness over time.
* **Streak Management**: Stay motivated with daily streaks for consistency and goal pursuit.
* **Custom Reporting**: Look back at your history and monitor performance trends effortlessly.

### 💊 Holistic Routine Optimization
* **Supplement Tracking**: Log vitamins, minerals, nootropics, and custom compounds.
* **Wellness Journals**: Track your physical activities, sleep quality, and mood in one unified, cohesive interface.
* **Personalized Protocols**: Create custom plans tailored directly to your specific biology and longevity goals.

### 🔒 Privacy-First Architecture
Your health data is extremely sensitive, and we treat it as such.
* **Local-First Options**: Keep your data locally on your device using IndexedDB, reducing external tracking.
* **Secure Cloud Synchronization**: Seamlessly back up and restore data cross-device securely using Google Authentication and Firebase infrastructure.

### 📲 Cross-Platform Experience
Built with modern web technologies and packaged natively, BioScend provides a lightning-fast, highly responsive experience across both Web and Android platforms natively. 

---

### Known Issues & Minor Limitations
* *Syncing*: Offline tracking data may take a few seconds to synchronize with the cloud once a stable connection is re-established.
* *AI Analysis*: The Gemini AI engine requires a robust dataset to provide the most precise recommendations; the insights will heavily improve after 7 days of consistent logging.
